My 13-year-old told me I talk a lot. He said I do not listen to him.

He is right.

Many of our talks turn into disasters. We start with something small, like his phone. We end somewhere much bigger. Nothing gets fixed.

If you have a teenager, you probably know this exact fight.

I was trying to win the argument. I should have been trying to keep my son.

That one hurt to admit.

Here is what I am trying now. Not what worked. I do not know yet if it works. What I am trying.

We are writing a family agreement — together. Not rules I hand down. Rules we both shape. It covers the phone, chores, and what we do when we disagree. My son has not signed it yet. We are still talking about it. It is an experiment, and he gets a vote.

The hardest part for me is the listening part. When he talks, I want to fix it fast. I am learning to close my mouth and let him finish. I am bad at this. I am trying anyway.

I will be honest with you: this agreement might not fix our nights. But the fights were not fixing anything either.

If you are in the same fight in your house — you are not alone in it.

— Amam
