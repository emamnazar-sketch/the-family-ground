Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Who we are

The Family Ground (thefamilyground.com) is a family membership website run by Amam Nazar. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at thefamilyground@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Account information. When you sign up with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google. That's how we know who you are — we never see or store your Google password.

Information you enter about your family. To use the chore dashboards and family agreements, you may enter labels for your kids (we ask you to use ages, not real names), chore titles, family rules, rewards, and notes. This is the heart of the service, and it stays yours.

Photos. If you turn on photo proof for a chore, your kid can upload a photo when the chore is done. You see it when you review the chore.

Automatic information. Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical data (such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited) to keep the site secure and working.

Payment information. When memberships open, payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number — Stripe handles that directly.

3. Children

The Family Ground is made for parents. Children do not create accounts, do not provide email addresses, and do not sign up on their own. A child uses the site only through a parent's account — typically by scanning a parent-generated QR code that signs them in anonymously, with no personal information required.

Any information about a child on the site (such as an age label or a chore photo) is entered by the parent, and the parent is responsible for it. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information directly, contact us and we will delete it.

4. How we use your information

  • To run your account: dashboards, chores, agreements, and game missions.
  • To communicate with you about your membership (for example, trial reminders or payment receipts).
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.
  • To improve the service, using aggregated, non-identifying patterns — never by reading your family's private content for advertising.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use your family's data to train advertising models.

5. Who processes your data

We rely on a small number of trusted service providers to run the site. They only process your data to provide their service to us:

  • Google — sign-in (OAuth). Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase — database and authentication hosting.
  • Cloudflare — website hosting and security.
  • Stripe — payment processing, when memberships open. Subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety and rights of our users and the service.

6. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and browser storage entries strictly to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we must keep limited records for legal or accounting reasons (for example, payment receipts).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including rights under laws like the GDPR and California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to:

  • See what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your personal information.
  • Ask for a copy of your data.
  • Opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information (we don't sell or share it, but the right still applies).

To use any of these rights, email thefamilyground@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. You can also delete most of your family data yourself, right from your dashboard.

9. Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls so members can only see their own family's data, and private storage for uploaded photos. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps appropriate to the sensitivity of family information.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you by email or with a notice on the site before the change takes effect.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email thefamilyground@gmail.com and we'll answer.
