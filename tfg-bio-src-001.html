Amam Nazar is a father of six — ages 9 to 21 — learning how to keep his family close in a distracted world.

"Parenting is a privilege, and not everyone gets the chance. It's the most sacred job in the world, one where parents sacrifice for their children without a second thought. We give our time, our sleep, and often our own dreams, and we rarely stop to count the cost."

"But it isn't always easy. It comes with hard days, quiet doubts, and moments that truly terrify us. In a world that competes for our children's attention every second, raising them with values can feel like an uphill road."

You don't have to walk it alone. The Family Ground is a place to share the struggles, trade what's working, and remind each other why we do this. Let's come together and raise the next Gandhis and Mandelas: children of courage, compassion, and conscience.

He is also writing a memoir, Between the Two World.
