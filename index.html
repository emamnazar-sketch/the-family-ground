Questions parents ask

What do I get as a member?

You get the chore and routine tracker, the family agreement builder, the 7-Day Family Screen Reset course, and every new course as it's released. Everything lives in one simple dashboard.

How much does it cost?

. No hidden fees, no surprises.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel in one click from your account, and you keep full access until the end of your billing period.

Is this for single parents?

Absolutely. The tools work for any family — one parent, two parents, grandparents raising kids. If you're the one running the house, this is for you.

Do my kids need their own accounts?

No. You manage everything from your own account. When you set up chores and routines, use age labels like "Age 9" — never your kids' real names.

I'm not tech-savvy — is this hard to use?

No. If you can send a text message, you can use this. Everything is plain and simple, and if you ever get stuck, you can reach me for help.
